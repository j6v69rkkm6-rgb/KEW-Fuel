FORMULAS WE USE
Halfway Distance Total Trip Distance ÷ 2
Halfway Fuel Fuel On Board − (Current Burn × Minutes to Halfway ÷ 60)
HWP Required Fuel (Current Burn × Minutes from HWP to Destination ÷ 60) + 70 descent + Alternate + 350 reserve
HWP Fuel Margin Halfway Fuel − HWP Required Fuel
Predicted Landing Fuel Fuel On Board − (Current Burn × Minutes to Destination ÷ 60) + 70 descent fuel
Predicted Landing Weight ZFW + Predicted Landing Fuel
Take-Off Vr Automatically estimated from take-off weight and selected flap schedule
Landing Quick-Reference VAPP Automatically estimated from landing weight + selected flap setting
Take-Off Quick Estimate Weight + pressure altitude + OAT + wind + slope + flaps → estimated distance and runway margin
Landing Quick Estimate Landing weight + pressure altitude + OAT + wind + slope + flaps → estimated distance and runway margin
Round Trip Fuel Required (44 + Fuel to TOD + 70) × 2 + Alternate + 350
Use current APEX fuel burn and Air Trail planned values. Always verify against company SOP/COM and aircraft documents.
